## Overview

A healthcare management and recommender system, described in the repository as "AI-enhanced healthcare diagnostics and management system inspired by ZK Medical Billing Platform". It targets inefficiencies in current healthcare diagnostics and management by applying AI models that analyze patient data for more accurate and timely diagnosis and offer predictive insights for preventive care, with the stated aims of reducing human error and improving decision-making.

The project runs on localhost, and the frontend is deployed at [healthy002.netlify.app](https://healthy002.netlify.app/).

## How it's built

Technologies used, as listed in the README:

- **Backend / frontend:** Django, React JS, SQLite
- **Data and ML:** Pandas, XGBoost, explainable AI via SHAP
- **LLM:** Gemini
- **Imaging and documents:** OpenCV, Tesseract OCR
- **Visualisation:** Matplotlib
