## Status

Built for **WeMakeDevs × AWS Environmental Hacks 2026** (Air track), during the 8–11 Oct 2026 hack
window. **Scored 27/30.**

## The problem

Every October morning a Delhi resident checks one number before a run, a walk or a ride. That number
has no age, no source and no error bar, and it is often old: on 4 Oct 2026 the mirror most people use
showed one station 31 hours stale and another 56 hours stale. Last season's loudest complaint was not
*"what is the AQI?"* but *"can I trust it?"*

## What it does

- **One plain answer** in Hindi or English: go ahead, wait for the air to improve, or stay in, from a
  fixed, published rule on the CPCB AQI bands.
- **The age of every number.** Each answer says how many hours ago the station measured it, and where
  it came from.
- **An honest range when it is old.** Past 3 hours, the app shows *"AQI probably 232–264"*: a
  split-conformal interval from the 3 nearest live stations plus bias-corrected CAMS. Advice uses the
  top of the range.
- **Hold me to this.** One tap starts an AWS Step Functions execution that waits, at no compute cost,
  until that station reports again, then shows whether the real number landed inside the range.
- **A public track record.** Every station, every morning: right or missed, with misses shown as
  plainly as hits against an 80% coverage target.
- **For schools**, a 06:30 plan for assembly, PE and recess from the school's nearest station.

## Measured

A backtest replaying the Oct–Nov 2025 smog season from the OpenAQ archive, simulating 1–6 hour silent
outages across 14 stations: 55,944 evaluations, **80.3%** empirical coverage against the 80% target,
and a mean absolute error of **12.8** for the range's estimate against **25.8** for "same as the last
reading", a 50.4% reduction. The backend carries 88 tests.

The stack is serverless on AWS: EventBridge Scheduler, Lambda, DynamoDB, Step Functions, Polly for a
spoken Hindi answer, and an hourly zero-shot Chronos-Bolt forecast that never changes the verdict.

**The limits, as the repository states them.** It is station air, not your street, and an estimate,
not the truth. No school has used the school plan yet; what it would change is described, not
measured.
