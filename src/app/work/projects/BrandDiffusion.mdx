## Overview

BrandDiffusion is an AI-powered design system that generates marketing visuals with brand
compliance and cultural awareness. Rather than a single flat image, it outputs layered, editable
design components (background, subject, product, logo, and text metadata) compatible with Adobe
Express.

- Uses intent detection to identify whether a campaign is a product launch, festival greeting, or
  sale, then applies the appropriate creative controls.
- Preserves exact product fidelity and integrates cultural context for festivals.
- Generates professional layouts with accurate text rendering for headlines, subheadlines, and
  CTAs.
- Built with Stable Diffusion XL, ControlNet, CLIP, GFPGAN, Llama-3, MediaPipe, and a FastAPI
  backend, shipped as an Adobe Express add-on.

**Winner — "Most Valuable Feedback for Developer Tool Usage"**, Adobe Express Add-ons for
Enterprise Hackathon. Built with Anshika Singh.
