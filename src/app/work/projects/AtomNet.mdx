## Overview

AtoM-Net is a cognitive neuro-symbolic pipeline for negotiation theory of mind, combining
neural belief inference with a fully symbolic decision engine.

- **Stage 1 — Atomic Inference:** LLM-based semantic parsing of cheap talk and deterministic
  parsing of costly signals to produce raw belief/preference probabilities.
- **Stage 2 — Economic Consistency Validator:** A symbolic contradiction function refines raw
  beliefs, applying a decay factor and penalizing deception risk.
- **Stage 3 — Symbolic Decision Engine:** Exhaustive deal-space enumeration (non-LLM/symbolic)
  with expected-utility maximization to generate the optimal offer.
- **Stage 4 — Reflexion Layer:** Inter-game learning via LLM self-critique, updating beliefs,
  acceptance probability, and utility estimates across games.

Work is currently **in review at EMNLP 2026**.
