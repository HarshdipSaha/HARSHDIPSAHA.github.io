## Intent

Owner feedback after the rebuild, verbatim in spirit: "UI is great, keep it the same, but UX is
terrible — no one understands what I do or what World Rank 3 is; /process is opaque and should not
lead with Optum; there is no way back to the home page from other routes except the footer; the
brain section's text overlaps the image." A friend's review confirmed the last two.

## Notes

- `.label` at 45 % paper measures 3.82:1 on ink at 11–13 px, below AA for text that size. Where a
  label carries meaning it should be lifted to ≥ 60 %; left for a follow-up because the owner pinned
  the incumbent look and the fix touches every eyebrow on the site.
- The "Home" pill's label is a literal in `Nav.tsx` rather than an entry in `nav`, because the
  footer already lists Home and `sitemap.ts` derives its route list from `nav`. One deliberate
  exception to copy-in-`site.ts`.
- impeccable's critique step ran degraded: the two assessment sub-agents it spawned reported after
  it had been told to proceed, so it evaluated inline first; both reports were incorporated by the
  coordinator.
